Content Strategy for AI Visibility

AI models favor content that is direct, well-structured, and authoritative. Our content must:

  • Lead with direct answers — question-based headings with 40-60 word answers before expanding
  • Use semantic HTML — proper heading hierarchy, lists, tables (AI crawlers parse these easily)
  • Avoid heavy JavaScript — SSG ensures all content is in HTML (critical for AI crawling)
  • Include location + entity references naturally throughout content
  • Build topical authority — comprehensive coverage of all electrical topics signals expertise
  • Maintain FAQ sections on every service page with schema markup

AI Visibility Rule

"AI visibility is fueled by traditional SEO strength." Every signal that helps Google rankings also helps LLM recommendations. The difference is that AI models also weight brand mentions without links, review sentiment analysis, and structured data more heavily than traditional search.

Review Request System

  1. Job Completion: Technician marks job as complete in CRM
  2. Automated SMS (2 hours later): "Hi [Name], thank you for choosing GP Electric! We'd love your feedback. Please leave us a quick Google review: [direct link]. It means a lot to our small business!"
  3. Follow-Up Email (24 hours): If no review, send email version with same link
  4. Thank-You Response: Owner responds to every review within 24 hours

Review Response Templates

Positive Review Response

"Thank you so much, [Name]! We're glad we could help with your [service type] in [area]. Our team takes pride in delivering quality electrical work for every customer. We'll be here whenever you need us! — The GP Electric Team"

Negative Review Response

"[Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience. This isn't up to our standards. Please reach out to us directly at (780) 882-3046 so we can make this right. Customer satisfaction is our top priority. — [Owner Name], GP Electric"

Additional Review Generation Tactics

  • QR Code on Invoices: Physical QR code linking directly to Google review page
  • In-Person Ask: Train technicians to ask satisfied customers for reviews at job completion
  • Email Signature: "Love our work? Leave us a Google review!" with link
  • Website Review Page: Dedicated page with Google review button
  • Social Media: Share positive reviews on Facebook/LinkedIn (builds social proof + encourages others)
  • Past Customer Outreach: Contact previous satisfied customers for reviews (one-time batch)
Important: Ethical Review Practices

Never offer incentives for reviews (violates Google guidelines). Never post fake reviews. Focus on making it easy for genuinely satisfied customers to share their experience. Consistency matters more than spikes — 3-5 reviews per month is sustainable and looks natural to Google.

Review Monitoring

  • Set up Google Alerts for "Grande Prairie Electric" mentions
  • Monitor Google, Facebook, Yelp, and HomeStars weekly
  • Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
  • Track review velocity and rating trends in CRM dashboard
