## TARGET AUDIENCE SEGMENTS (Who We're Going After)

### Tier 1 — Quick Wins (Highest urgency, fastest revenue)

**1. Emergency Residential**
- Homeowner at -40C whose furnace circuit just died
- Panel sparking, breaker won't reset, power out in half the house
- **Why quick win:** They're not shopping around. They call whoever shows up first.
- **Website play:** Dedicated emergency page, click-to-call, "We Answer 24/7" messaging
- **Hormozi angle:** Reduce time delay to zero. "On-site in 60 minutes or the service call is free"

**2. New Home Builders / General Contractors**
- 700+ housing starts happening RIGHT NOW
- Builders need reliable electrical subs who show up on time
- **Why quick win:** One relationship = dozens of jobs per year
- **Website play:** Dedicated "For Builders" page with commercial focus
- **Hormozi angle:** Dream 100 — identify the top 20 builders in GP, reach out personally

**3. Panel Upgrade Homeowners**
- Older GP homes (1970s-2000s) running 100A panels can't handle modern loads
- Adding hot tub, EV charger, shop heater = panel upgrade required
- **Why quick win:** High ticket ($3,000-$6,000+), no competitor has a dedicated page
- **Website play:** Dedicated panel upgrade page with "Signs You Need an Upgrade" content
- **Hormozi angle:** Free electrical health check (loss leader that converts to panel upgrades)

### Tier 2 — High Volume (Steady pipeline builders)

**4. Farm & Acreage Owners (Peace Country)**
- New shops, barns, grain bins, yard sites all need full electrical service
- Underground service runs, shop wiring, grain dryer circuits, yard lighting
- Beaverlodge, Sexsmith, Wembley, Clairmont — agricultural communities
- **Why high volume:** Constant year-round demand, large jobs ($5,000-$50,000+)
- **Website play:** Dedicated "Farm & Acreage Electrical" page — NO competitor has this
- **Hormozi angle:** "Complete Shop Electrical Package" — service, lighting, outlets, panel, all-in-one quote

**5. Small Town Residents (Surrounding Communities)**
- Clairmont, Sexsmith, Beaverlodge, Wembley, Hythe, Spirit River, Valleyview, Fairview, Peace River
- Limited or zero local electrician options in most of these towns
- **Why high volume:** Service area pages = easy SEO wins, low competition
- **Website play:** Individual service area page for each town with local content
- **Hormozi angle:** "Your Local Electrician, Even When You're Not in the City"

**6. Renovation / Upgrade Homeowners**
- Kitchen renos, basement developments, bathroom upgrades, lighting modernization
- Smart home installations, USB outlets, recessed lighting
- **Why high volume:** Smaller jobs but constant flow
- **Website play:** Residential services page with specific sub-services listed

### Tier 3 — Big Money (Longer sales cycle, highest value)

**7. Commercial Businesses**
- Retail stores, restaurants, offices, warehouses in GP
- Tenant improvements, new construction, lighting retrofits
- GP is the retail hub for 300,000+ people in NW Alberta
- **Website play:** Commercial services page with business-specific case studies

**8. Industrial / Oil & Gas**
- Well pads, compressor stations, plant electrical, instrumentation
- CNRL and Paramount both actively drilling and building in the area
- **Why big money:** Single contracts can be $100K+
- **Website play:** Industrial services page, certifications front and center

**9. EV Charger / Solar / Green Energy**
- EV charger installs with 46% government rebate (commercial)
- Solar installations with federal 20-30% tax credit (farms/businesses)
- New CEC code rules for EV and solar
- **Why big money:** Growing market, government money subsidizing the sale
- **Website play:** Dedicated EV charger page + solar page, lead with rebate numbers

## THE GRAND SLAM OFFER (Hormozi Framework)

### Value Equation Applied
```
Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort)
```

**Dream Outcome:** Safe, beautiful, code-compliant electrical work that protects your family and increases your property value. For commercial: zero-downtime electrical that keeps your business running and making money.

**Perceived Likelihood (Stack the Proof):**
- Google reviews (goal: 25 in 90 days, 100 in 12 months)
- Before/after photo gallery on every service page
- Video testimonials (raw iPhone style, not polished)
- Licensed, bonded, insured badges everywhere
- Specific numbers: "X homes serviced in Grande Prairie"
- Customer stories from their specific neighborhood/town

**Time Delay (Reduce to Zero):**
- Same-day estimates
- 24/7 emergency response
- "We show up when we say we will" — on-time guarantee
- Online quote request form (instant acknowledgment)
- Speed to contact: respond within 5 minutes (average business takes 42 hours)

**Effort & Sacrifice (Make It Dead Simple):**
- Online booking / quote request
- No phone tag — we call YOU back
- Clear pricing guidance (not vague "call for quote")
- Clean job sites — "We leave it cleaner than we found it"
- One point of contact throughout the project

### The Irresistible Offers (by segment)

**Residential — "Complete Home Electrical Health Check"**
- Free 30-minute inspection of panel, outlets, smoke detectors, GFCI
- Provide written safety report with photos
- This is the LOSS LEADER that converts to panel upgrades, rewiring, outlet upgrades
- Hormozi: "Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no"

**Farm/Acreage — "Complete Shop Electrical Package"**
- Full service entrance, panel, lighting, outlets, and 240V circuits — one price
- Include free surge protector with every shop build
- Bonus: yard light installation included with any shop electrical over $X

**Emergency — "60-Minute Response Guarantee"**
- If we don't arrive within 60 minutes, the service call fee is waived
- 24/7/365 — no answering service, a real electrician answers
- This is THE differentiator — nobody else in GP is making this promise

**Commercial — "Zero Downtime Electrical"**
- We schedule around YOUR business hours
- After-hours and weekend work at no premium for contract clients
- Annual maintenance plan option

### Three-Tier Pricing (Every Service)

| Tier | Name | What's Included | Purpose |
|------|------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | Essential | The core service, done right | Entry point, gets them in the door |
| 2 | Premium | Core + extended warranty + priority scheduling | Where most people land (anchor effect) |
| 3 | Total Protection | Premium + annual inspection + 24/7 priority line + surge protection | 10-15% take this, highest margin |

### Service Pages — Each One Is a Mini Sales Page

Every service page follows this structure:
1. **Hook headline** — outcome-focused, not service-focused
   - Bad: "Panel Upgrade Services"
   - Good: "Is Your Home's Electrical Panel a Fire Hazard? Let's Fix That."
2. **Problem → Solution** — 2-3 paragraphs agitating the pain, then presenting GP Electric as the answer
3. **What's included** — clear scope of what they get
4. **Pricing guidance** — not exact prices, but ranges so they know what to expect
5. **Before/after photos** — from real GP Electric jobs
6. **FAQ section** — addresses objections, includes FAQ schema for SEO
7. **Testimonial** — from a customer who got THIS specific service
8. **CTA** — "Get a Free Quote for Your [Service]" with form

### Service Area Pages — Local SEO Dominators

Each of the 10 towns gets its own page with UNIQUE content:
- H1: "Licensed Electrician in [Town] — GP Electric Inc"
- Local context (what makes this community unique, population, growth)
- Services available in that area
- Response time from GP office
- Local testimonials (when available)
- FAQ specific to that area
- Embedded Google Map
- Full LocalBusiness schema with GeoCoordinates
- CTA: "Get a Free Quote for Your [Town] Project"

**These are the easiest SEO wins.** Nobody has "electrician Sexsmith" or "electrician Beaverlodge" pages. We'll rank on page 1 for these within weeks.
