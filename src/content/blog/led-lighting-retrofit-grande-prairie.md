## Do I need a permit for pot light installation in Grande Prairie?

Most electrical work in Alberta requires a permit through the local Safety Codes authority. In Grande Prairie the permit is issued by the City’s Building Services office and can be applied for online at alberta.ca/obtain-an-electrical-permit. The Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) governs the installation of recessed cans, pot lights and dimmer circuits, and a permit ensures that a certified inspector will sign off on the work. Skipping the permit can void insurance coverage and may cause problems when you sell the property, especially on acreages that use well or pressure systems.

## What type of housing should I choose for recessed lighting in a new build versus a retrofit?

Recessed housings come in two main categories: new construction cans and retrofit (remodel) cans. New construction cans attach to the roof joists before the drywall goes up, and they can be insulated around the fixture to meet the thermal requirements of a cold climate. Retrofit cans clip into existing ceiling openings and are ideal for upgrading older shops on 100th Street or finishing a basement in Clairmont. For any area that may see moisture such as a bathroom near a basement waterproofing project or a shop with a leaky roof, choose a sealed housing rated for damp locations. Insulation contact ratings are especially important in acreages where attic space is limited and you want to avoid fire hazards.

When you plan the layout, sketch the positions on a piece of paper and mark where the switch will be located. This helps the electrician determine the length of wire runs and the number of circuit breakers needed.

## Why do some LED bulbs buzz with dimmers and how can I stop it?

Buzzing or flickering usually means the dimmer switch is not fully compatible with the LED driver. Traditional leading‑edge dimmers were designed for incandescent loads and can cause a high‑pitched hum when paired with modern LEDs. The solution is to install a trailing‑edge or electronic low‑voltage (ELV) dimmer that is listed for LED
